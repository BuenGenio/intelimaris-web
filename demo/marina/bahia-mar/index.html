A real scan, a different marina

The point cloud is a real aerial LiDAR survey, but of Halifax Harbor in Daytona Beach, not Bahia Mar: it is here as a sample of what the viewer does with survey data. The listing facts are Bahia Mar’s, compiled from the operator’s public site. Nothing on this page is a live feed.
