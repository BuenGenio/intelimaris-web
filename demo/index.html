Explore the demos

Try a part of your day on the water.

Plan a sample passage, compare berths, work a marina day board or explore the product screens. Each demo explains what you can change and where its information comes from.

Most examples use sample data. The conditions demo uses public feeds, and the survey viewers use recorded aerial data. Choose a starting point below, or find the guide for your role.

What would you like to try?

Interactive example.

Plan a sample passage

Switch between sample vessels or adjust their draft and height. Watch the route update or explain why it cannot proceed, then try a different departure or destination.

Routes use a simplified map and illustrative depth and bridge limits. They are not navigation guidance.

Simulated sensor.

Watch a reading get older

Pause the sample sensor or cut its connection. Watch the age and status change, then restore the link.

Values, connection changes and predictions are examples. Time runs faster here; no vessel is connected.

Sample berth data.

Find a berth that fits

Choose a sample vessel and day. Open a berth to see its approach details or the reason it does not fit.

The Bahia Mar layout, occupancy, depths and limits are illustrative. This demo does not reserve a berth.

Interactive example.

Draw a dock. Work the day board.

Draw a dock and add berths, or load the sample layout. Mark a sample arrival and see the berth state change.

Edits and arrivals stay in this demo. They do not change a real marina, publish a dock listing or make a booking.

Simulated reporting.

See how a hazard can change a route

Place a sample hazard on the chart. Watch the route respond, then confirm or clear the report.

These reports and route changes stay in the demo. Hazard viewing exists in WaterWayz; report, confirm and clear actions are still in development.

Recorded survey + sample berths.

Explore a recorded marina scan

Rotate the survey, change the view and open a sample berth to read its approach details.

The real scan is from Halifax Harbor. The Bahia Mar berth overlay is a separate illustration, not a survey of those berths or a live occupancy feed.

Public feeds + calculated times.

Check the conditions nearby

Choose one of six marinas. Compare the tide prediction, available wind observation and sunset time, with sources and observation ages.

NOAA supplies tide predictions and NWS supplies weather observations where available. Sunset is calculated. Nearby stations may differ from conditions at the berth.

The vessel, traffic, conditions and arrival are sample data. Hazard and emergency controls do not submit reports or contact emergency services.

Recorded aerial survey

Inspect the full point cloud

Rotate, zoom and compare height, intensity and structure views of Halifax Harbor. The browser displays about 1.18 million points selected from a 220.4-million-point survey.

The surrounding Bahia Mar listing is a sample. Structure views are derived from the survey; this is not live berth occupancy or a measurement of underwater depth.
