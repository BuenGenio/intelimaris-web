InteliMarina

When the owner is away, the marina is still in the loop.

Fires, sinkings, toxic fumes, and thermal-runaway events rarely wait for the owner to be aboard. The shore-side layer turns isolated onboard telemetry into real-time marina awareness with the context staff need to act.

Escalation path

1

Smoke, CO, water ingress, or battery anomaly is detected onboard.

2

Nearby gateway coverage preserves transmission and forwards context.

3

4
